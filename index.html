Hi, welcome to my personal website! I'm currently a PhD fellow working with Siemens Healthineers and the Center for Mind/Brain Sciences on the translation of advanced diffusion MRI methods in 3T Siemens scanners.

Supervised by Prof. Jorge Jovicich, my research focuses on implementing Correlation Tensor MRI on clinical 3T Prisma scanners to disentangle the biophysical origins of diffusional kurtosis in the human brain. Using double diffusion encoding, CTI provides novel contrasts for cell-level changes like dendritic beading and neuronal swelling observed in the "silent" stages of dementia and stroke. Further, my research also focuses on devloping real-time preprocessing techniques for DWI within the Siemens OpenRecon framework.
